合作方向 Ways to participate

找到一件，
值得一起完成的事。 Find something
worth creating together.

01

地方共創與公共展演 Community co-creation

支持歌曲、音樂劇、工作坊或地方節慶，讓學生與居民共同參與企劃、創作與展演。 Support songs, musical theatre, workshops, or local festivals created and performed by students and residents.

適合：希望建立地方連結與公共參與的夥伴。 For partners seeking community connections and public participation.

02

文化內容與數位紀錄 Cultural content and documentation

支持 Podcast、地方訪談、影像、教材或數位導覽，讓地方故事留下可以持續使用的形式。 Support podcasts, interviews, films, learning materials, or digital interpretation that give local stories a lasting form.

適合：關注文化保存與內容共創的夥伴。 For partners interested in cultural preservation and co-created content.

03

青年培力與創作支持 Learning through creation

以地方或永續議題連結課程與學生團隊，支持實作、創作與作品呈現。 Connect local or sustainability themes with courses and student teams, supporting practice, creation, and showcases.

適合：希望投入教育與人才培育的夥伴。 For partners committed to education and student development.

04

員工參與與地方走讀 Employee engagement

以文化走讀、共融體驗或工作坊，讓員工親身認識地方，參與彼此交流的過程。 Invite employees into guided walks, inclusive experiences, and workshops to learn from local communities.

適合：希望把永續行動帶進組織日常的夥伴。 For partners bringing sustainability into everyday organizational life.

合作範圍、期程、預算與成果使用方式，依雙方需求共同討論。 Scope, timeline, budget, and use of the resulting materials are agreed together.

一起走多遠 The shape of a partnership

從一次參與，
到長期同行。 From a first step
to a lasting relationship.

先談希望留下的成果，再選擇合適的參與深度。 Start with what you hope to leave behind, then choose the right level of involvement.

01

單次支持 A first step

地方走讀、工作坊或單場活動。 A guided walk, workshop, or single event.

02

深度共創 Co-create

共同完成歌曲、Podcast、影像或教材。 Create songs, podcasts, films, or learning materials together.

03

年度夥伴 An ongoing partnership

持續參與地方共創，整理年度作品與行動紀錄。 Support ongoing creation, with a record of the year’s works and activities.
