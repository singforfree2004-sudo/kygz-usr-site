為什麼是西港 Why Sigang

一座地方，
有許多種聲音。 One place.
Many voices.

西港有慶安宮香科、胡麻產業、地方歌謠與社區記憶。這些文化不是放在玻璃櫃裡的標本，而是仍在生活裡延續的日常。我們想做的，是讓年輕學習者、孩子、居民與外來的朋友，不只是觀看西港，而是能夠走進西港、聽見西港，也把自己的聲音放進西港。 Sigang holds temple rituals, sesame agriculture, local songs, and community memory. These are not objects behind glass; they are still breathing in everyday life. Our work invites young learners, children, residents, and visitors not only to observe Sigang, but to enter it, listen to it, and add their own voices to it.

對我們來說，社會共融不是把人請來坐在台下，而是讓不同背景的人能說出自己的故事、參與創作、留下聲音，甚至站上舞台。當一首歌開始有人跟著唱，地方文化也就有了新的共同作者。 For us, inclusion is not simply inviting people to sit in the audience. It is giving people from different backgrounds the chance to tell stories, create, leave a voice, and sometimes step onto the stage. When a song begins to be sung together, local culture gains new co-authors.

作品型成果 Creative Outputs

歌曲、Podcast、紀錄片、音樂劇、電子書與 AR 導覽，讓地方故事有了可以被聽見、觀看、分享與再使用的形式。 Songs, podcasts, documentaries, musical theatre, ebooks, and AR interpretation give local stories forms that can be heard, watched, shared, and reused.

社會影響型成果 Social Impact

學生被場域培力，居民成為共同作者，孩子重新認識家鄉，地方文化也透過展演、獎項與國際交流被更多人看見。 Students are trained through the field, residents become co-authors, children rediscover home, and local culture reaches wider audiences through performance, recognition, and international exchange.

Performance

音樂劇與成果展 Musical Theatre and Public Showcase

音樂劇把田野裡聽見的人、事與地方情感編成一場共同演出。當學生與居民站在同一個作品裡，西港文化也被重新看見。 Musical theatre gathers field stories, people, and local feeling into a shared performance. When students and residents stand inside the same work, Sigang is seen anew.

Music

西港原創歌曲 Songs of Sigang

歌曲讓地方記憶有了旋律，也讓故事更容易被帶走、被想起、被下一個人接著唱。 Songs give local memory a melody, making stories easier to carry, remember, and sing forward.

Fieldwork

地方共創與教育再使用 Co-creation and Educational Reuse

共創歌曲與地方教材走進學校、節慶與社區活動。文化不只是被保存，而是被孩子重新使用，成為他們理解家鄉的一種方式。 Co-created songs and local materials move through schools, festivals, and community events. Culture is not only preserved; it is reused by younger generations as a way to understand home.

Digital

Podcast、紀錄片與 AR 轉譯 Podcast, Documentary, and AR Translation

聲音、影像、電子書與 AR 導覽，讓地方故事不只留在一次活動裡，而能在更多時間、更多地方繼續被遇見。 Audio, video, ebooks, and AR interpretation allow local stories to live beyond a single event and be encountered again elsewhere.

給想深入了解的你 A closer look

計畫如何實踐：地方、課程與共創方法 How it works: place, curriculum, and co-creation ＋

計畫邏輯 Project Logic

我們不是只到西港辦一場活動，而是陪地方累積可以被傳下去的聲音。 We are not simply bringing events to Sigang. We are helping local voices grow into forms that can keep travelling.

地方記憶 Local memory + 音樂創作 Music creation + 數位轉譯 Digital translation + 共融展演 Inclusive performance = 西港的新共同作者 New co-authors of Sigang
01

從地方問題出發 Start From Local Questions

西港有香科、胡麻、歌謠與社區記憶，也面對青年參與下降、傳統文化傳承斷層、陣頭文化刻板印象與地方特色需要重新被理解的挑戰。 Sigang carries temple rituals, sesame culture, songs, and community memory, while also facing declining youth participation, cultural gaps, stereotypes around troupes, and the need to tell its identity anew.

02

把大學專業帶進場域 Bring University Practice Into the Field

流行音樂產業系把課程、企劃、聲音製作、影像紀錄與展演實務帶到西港，讓學生在真實場域裡學習，也讓專業回應地方需要。 Popular Music Industry brings curriculum, planning, sound production, documentation, and performance practice into Sigang, so students learn in real settings and skills respond to local needs.

03

讓不同的人一起創作 Create With Different People

居民、孩子、長者、青農、宮廟、學校與學生一起訪談、寫歌、排練、錄音與演出。共融不只是被邀請觀看，而是在作品裡留下自己的聲音。 Residents, children, elders, young farmers, temples, schools, and students interview, write songs, rehearse, record, and perform together. Inclusion means leaving one’s own voice inside the work.

04

把成果變成可延續的方法 Turn Works Into a Repeatable Method

歌曲、Podcast、紀錄片、音樂劇、電子書與 AR 導覽，讓地方故事離開一次活動，繼續在學校、節慶、舞台、網路與國際交流裡被遇見。 Songs, podcasts, documentaries, musical theatre, ebooks, and AR interpretation let local stories move beyond one event and continue through schools, festivals, stages, online platforms, and international exchange.

為什麼是流行音樂產業系 Why Popular Music Industry

音樂不是活動的背景聲，而是讓人靠近彼此的方法。 Music is not background sound. It is a way for people to move closer to one another.

課程成為實作培力 Curriculum as Practice-Based Learning

學生把課堂裡的演出、企劃、和聲、燈光音響與舞台實務帶進西港。專業不只停在考試和作業裡，而是在真實的人與地方之間被練習、被看見。 Students bring performance, planning, harmony, lighting, sound, and stage practice into Sigang. Their skills are not left inside assignments; they are sharpened through real people and real places.

地方故事變成可流通作品 Local Stories Become Public Works

一次訪談可以成為 Podcast，一段記憶可以寫進歌詞，一個地方符號也能走進影像、電子書、AR 導覽與公開展演。文化因此有了新的傳遞方式。 An interview can become a podcast, a memory can become a lyric, and a local symbol can travel through video, ebooks, AR interpretation, and public performance.

共融在共同完成作品中發生 Inclusion Happens Through Co-creation

學生、居民、孩子與長者不是被安排在旁邊觀看的人。他們在排練、訪談、創作與演出裡一起出力，也一起成為作品的一部分。 Students, residents, children, and elders are not placed at the side as viewers. Through rehearsal, interviews, creation, and performance, they become part of the work itself.

跨國共創的延伸 Cross-border co-creation

從西港出發，把地方共創帶到更遠的地方。 From Sigang, carrying local co-creation further outward.

回看既有 STUST × NYP 合作影片 ↗ Revisit the STUST × NYP collaboration film ↗

114-116 年第四期計畫將延續地方平台、課程實作與數位文化轉譯，並深化與新加坡 Nanyang Polytechnic (NYP) 的合作。這段交流由資傳系與流行音樂產業系共同推動，鄭靜怡主任攜手流行音樂系學生，把西港的田野經驗、聲音創作與地方敘事帶進跨國合作現場。西港不是被複製的案例，而是一個持續發展的方法：讓音樂、地方文化、數位媒體與高等教育，在跨國交流中找到新的共創節奏。 The 2025-2027 phase continues the local platform, curriculum pipeline, and digital cultural translation model while deepening collaboration with Nanyang Polytechnic (NYP), Singapore. Led through collaboration between Communication and Information Technology and Popular Music Industry, Director Ching-Yi Cheng worked with PMI students to bring Sigang's fieldwork, sound-making, and local storytelling into an international exchange setting. Sigang is not a case to be copied; it is a growing method for music, local culture, digital media, and higher education to find new rhythms of cross-border co-creation.
