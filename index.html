南臺科技大學「跨樂尬陣」從臺南西港出發，讓學生、居民、學童、長者、青農、地方組織與國際夥伴共同創作，把地方故事轉化為歌曲、Podcast、紀錄片、音樂劇、AR 導覽與公共展演。 Inclusive Harmony in Sigang is a music-led social inclusion project by Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology. It transforms local stories into songs, podcasts, documentaries, musical theatre, AR interpretation, and public co-creation.

為什麼是西港 Why Sigang

問題不是西港沒有文化，而是誰有機會參與文化的再創造。 The challenge is not a lack of culture, but who gets to co-author it.

西港有慶安宮香科、胡麻產業、地方歌謠與社區記憶，但許多年輕學習者、居民與外部觀眾，常把這些文化視為距離自己很遠的傳統。計畫的核心問題是：如何讓地方文化從被觀看的資產，變成可共同創作、共同演出、共同傳承的生活平台？ Sigang has temple rituals, sesame agriculture, local songs, and community memory. Yet many younger learners and residents encounter these resources as distant heritage rather than living culture they can shape. The project asks how local culture can move from passive viewing to shared creation.

我們把社會共融定義為「文化參與」：不同背景的人能說出自己的故事、參與創作、站上舞台、留下聲音，並成為地方文化的共同作者。 We define social inclusion as cultural participation: the opportunity for different people to speak, create, interpret, perform, and carry local culture forward.

為什麼是流行音樂產業系 Why Popular Music Industry

音樂不是裝飾，是讓不同人共同參與的工作方法。 Music is not decoration. It is a method for participation.

課程成為人才管線Curriculum as a Talent Pipeline

演藝經紀、活動企劃、跨域展演、和聲、燈光音響與舞台實務，讓學生先建立專業能力，再進入場域與地方共同工作。Courses in artist management, event planning, performance practice, harmony singing, lighting, sound, and stage production prepare students for field-based collaboration.

地方故事變成可流通作品Local Stories Become Public Works

訪談可以成為 Podcast，記憶可以成為歌詞，文化符號可以成為影像、電子書、AR 導覽與公開展演。Interviews become podcasts, memories become lyrics, and cultural symbols become videos, ebooks, AR interpretation, and public performances.

共融在共同完成作品中發生Inclusion Happens Through Co-creation

學生、居民、學童、長者與不同背景參與者，不是被安排在旁邊觀看，而是在幕前幕後共同成為作品的一部分。Students, residents, schoolchildren, elders, and diverse participants become co-authors on stage and behind the scenes.

第四期方向Next Phase

從西港出發，測試亞太高教共創模式。From Sigang toward an Asia-Pacific higher education co-creation model.

114-116 年第四期計畫將延續地方平台、課程管線與數位文化轉譯，並深化與新加坡 Nanyang Polytechnic (NYP) 的合作。西港不只是單一地方案例，而是測試音樂、地方文化、數位媒體與高等教育如何跨國共創的實驗場。The 2025-2027 phase continues the local platform, curriculum pipeline, and digital cultural translation model while deepening collaboration with Nanyang Polytechnic (NYP), Singapore. Sigang becomes a testing ground for cross-border co-creation through music, local culture, digital media, and higher education.
